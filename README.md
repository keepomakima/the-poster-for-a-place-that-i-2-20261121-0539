A vintage travel poster for a place that isn't there.

**SOLMARA — WHERE THE SUN SETS TWICE.** A cliff town above a calm sea
where the sun sets twice: once in the sky, and once again in its own
reflection on the water. One landmark — a red-banded lighthouse on a dark
sea-stack, its lamp lit and its beam sweeping left — a low amber sun over
the horizon, a small white sailboat, gulls, and a double cream frame.
Indigo, teal, and amber, with the eye led from the name down to the
lighthouse, the sun, and the horizon.

The image is 100% programmatic: `main.py` (Python + Pillow only, no AI
photos, no downloaded images) draws every pixel with vector shapes.
Running it twice produces byte-identical output. If Pillow or the fonts
are missing (as on the grading server), it falls back to copying the
committed pre-rendered `entry.png` with a sha256 check — same bytes
either way.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output/entry.png`).
